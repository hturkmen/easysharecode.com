# Google Ads Plan — EasyShareCode.com

## Target Audience
UK eVisa holders who need to store, manage, and quickly access their Home Office share codes.

## Campaign Settings
- **Location:** United Kingdom (priority: London, Manchester, Birmingham)
- **Device:** iOS only (Apple Wallet feature)
- **Schedule:** Weekdays 8:00-22:00
- **Daily Budget:** £10-20 (start)
- **Bid Strategy:** Maximize Conversions (App Install tracking)
- **Negative Keywords:** `free visa`, `visa application`, `home office contact`, `gov.uk login`, `apply for visa`, `visa fees`
